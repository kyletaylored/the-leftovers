The 2026 NXL World Cup ran from 11 to 15 November in Kissimmee, Florida, and the Leftovers put 24 squads on the results sheet across 13 divisions: X-Ball, 5v5, 3v3, 10v10, Classic Mounds and Youth.

## The Leftovers NOV: Novice 3v3 champions

Novice 3v3 had 68 teams on Sunday, and four of them were ours. The Leftovers NOV went into the playoffs as the 12th seed and didn't lose a match after that:

| Round | Beat | Score |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Top 32 | WRECKAG3 (#21) | 2–0 |
| Ochos | ATP Factory (#5) | 2–1 |
| Quarters | Port City Revelers (#29) | 2–0 |
| Semis | Abyssaly (#9) | 2–1 |
| Final | Rat Kings (#10) | 2–1 |

Three of those five went the distance. First place out of 68, on the biggest weekend of the season.

## Insane, 20-odd years later

The Leftovers Insane NOV was three old teammates from New Zealand who last played three-man together around 2002. They flew over to play with the Leftovers and picked up four wins from eight matches, finishing 44th in the same Novice bracket.
