## We were the leftovers

In spring 2022 a handful of people who played rec ball at the same field kept
running into the same problem: the local teams were full, the tryout dates
never worked, and the answer was always "maybe next season."

So we entered an event ourselves with whoever was free that Saturday. We went
2-3, stayed for all of it, and realized the barrier had never been skill. It
was a roster spot.

That's the name. We're the players who were left over, and we'd rather hand the
next person a slot than make them wait a season for one.

## How the roster actually works

There is no tryout, no dues, and no bench. Before each event we post the date
in the Discord and the Facebook group, and slots go to whoever confirms first
and pays their own entry. Some people play one event a year. Some have played
every event since 2022. Both count.

That churn is the point, not a problem to be solved. It means:

- **Anyone can get in.** Show up, cover your entry and paint, play the whole day.
- **Nobody sits.** If you confirmed, you're playing points.
- **New players get real reps.** Not a practice squad — actual tournament games.

## Where we play

We're based out of **Denton, Texas**, and run the PBLeague D4 circuit across
north Texas — Denton, Dallas, and Waco. We came up through D5 in 2025 and moved
up after a third-place finish at the Fall Classic.

## What we're not

We're not a pro team, we're not sponsored into six figures, and we're not going
to pretend the record is better than it is — it's all on the
[stats page](/stats), event by event, entered by hand after every tournament.

What we are is a team that exists so that people who want to play tournament
paintball can, without waiting to be picked.
