## Community-run group

**The Leftovers** is an informal, community-organized paintball group operated
by volunteer players. By using `theleftoverspb.com`, you understand and agree
that this site is provided for community coordination and informational
purposes.

## Squad join requests and tournament play

Submitting a [join request](/join) expresses interest in playing an event with
us and does **not** guarantee a roster spot until confirmed by a team
organizer.

- **Player responsibility:** Paintball is a physical sport with inherent risks.
  Every player is solely responsible for their own safety, equipment (marker,
  mask, air system, pods), travel, league memberships, event waivers, and
  insurance at any practice or tournament.
- **Shared costs and payment deadlines:** We do not charge recurring team dues.
  Confirmed players cover their own share of event entry, paint, and league fees
  as arranged directly with the squad organizer. Full payment for your share
  must be received by the stated event deadline for each event in order to be
  assigned to the active roster.

## Player Portal accounts

The Player Portal at `app.theleftoverspb.com` is where confirmed players
manage their profile, event requests and payments.

- **One account per person.** You can log in with an email link, or with
  Google, Facebook or Discord. Keep your email and connected accounts secure,
  because anyone who can use them can use your account.
- **Accurate details.** Use your real name and your own APPA player ID. A
  player ID can belong to only one profile. If yours is already taken, email us
  rather than creating another.
- **Under 18s.** Players under 18 don't get their own login. A parent or
  guardian manages their profile, and agrees to these terms for them.
- **Payments.** You pay your share off-site, using the methods shown for each
  event, with your reference code, and an organizer confirms it. Deadlines and
  refunds follow what's set out for each event.
- **Conduct.** Don't misuse another person's details or try to access what
  isn't yours. We may remove or block an account that does.
- **Leaving.** You can delete your account from your Account page at any time. See
  the [Privacy Policy](/privacy#player-portal) for what's kept.

## Jersey pre-orders and merchandise

From time to time we coordinate batch pre-orders for custom team jerseys or
apparel:

- **Off-site payment:** Pre-order forms on this site only record your sizing
  and customisation details (name/number). Payment is arranged off-site after
  you submit your request.
- **Custom items:** Because custom jerseys are made to order with your chosen
  name, number, and size, orders cannot be changed or refunded once the batch
  has been sent to production with the manufacturer.
