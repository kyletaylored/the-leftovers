## We were the leftovers

A handful of people who played rec ball at the same field kept running into the
same problem: the local teams were full, the tryout dates never worked, and the
answer was always "maybe next season."

So we entered an event ourselves with whoever was free that Saturday. We
realised the barrier had never been skill. It was a roster spot.

That's the name. We're the players who were left over, and we'd rather hand the
next person a slot than make them wait a season for one.

## How the roster actually works

There is no tryout, no dues, and no bench. Before each event we post the date
in the Discord and the Facebook page, and slots go to whoever confirms first
and pays their own entry. Some people play one event a year. Some have played
every event since the beginning. Both count.

That churn is the point, not a problem to be solved. It means:

- **Anyone can get in.** Show up, cover your entry and paint, play the whole day.
- **Nobody sits.** If you confirmed, you're playing points.
- **New players get real reps.** Not a practice squad — actual tournament games.

## What we're not

We're not a pro team, we're not sponsored into six figures, and we're not going
to pretend the record is better than it is — results go on the
[stats below](#stats) as they happen.

What we are is a team that exists so that people who want to play tournament
paintball can, without waiting to be picked.
